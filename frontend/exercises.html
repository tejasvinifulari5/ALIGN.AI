Exercise Guide

Squats
Lower Body · Beginner–Intermediate
Quadriceps Glutes Hamstrings Core
How to Perform
  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly turned out.
  2. Keep your chest up and back straight throughout the movement.
  3. Push your hips back and bend your knees to lower down.
  4. Lower until thighs are parallel to the floor (90° knee angle).
  5. Drive through your heels to return to standing position.
  6. Keep knees aligned over toes — do not let them cave inward.
Camera Setup
1
Place camera at hip height, 6–8 feet away.
2
Stand sideways — left side facing camera.
3
Ensure full body is visible — head to toe.
4
Good lighting — avoid backlighting.

Bicep Curls
Upper Body · Beginner
Biceps Forearms Shoulders
How to Perform
  1. Stand upright, feet shoulder-width apart, dumbbells at your sides.
  2. Keep elbows close to your torso throughout the movement.
  3. Curl the weights up by contracting your biceps.
  4. Raise until forearms are vertical and dumbbells near your shoulders.
  5. Slowly lower back to the starting position with control.
  6. Avoid swinging your body — use only your arms.

Pushups
Upper Body & Core · Beginner–Advanced
Chest Triceps Shoulders Core
How to Perform
  1. Start in a high plank — hands slightly wider than shoulder-width.
  2. Keep body in a straight line from head to heels.
  3. Lower your chest toward the floor by bending elbows.
  4. Go down until chest nearly touches the floor.
  5. Push back up to the starting position with full arm extension.
  6. Keep core tight throughout — don't let hips sag or rise.
Camera Setup
1

2
Camera should capture full body profile.
3
Ensure shoulder, elbow, wrist all visible.
4
Use a bright room for best detection.
